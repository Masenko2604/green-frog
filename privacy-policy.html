Personal data may be collected when you voluntarily submit information through our contact form or contact us directly using the contact details published on the website.

We process personal data for specific and legitimate purposes, including:

  • responding to your inquiry or request;
  • communicating with you regarding our services;
  • preparing information or an offer for requested services;
  • taking steps at your request before entering into a contract;
  • performing and managing contractual relationships;
  • complying with applicable legal obligations;
  • protecting our legitimate interests and legal rights;
  • maintaining the security and proper functioning of the website.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

The applicable legal basis depends on the particular purpose for which personal data is processed.

Depending on the circumstances, processing may be based on:

  • taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract;
  • performance of a contract;
  • compliance with a legal obligation;
  • our legitimate interests, where permitted by applicable law;
  • your consent, where consent is required by law.

We do not require consent where personal data can lawfully be processed on another legal basis.

6. Contact Form and EmailJS

Our website contains a contact form through which you can send an inquiry to us.

The form may collect your name, telephone number, email address and the content of your message.

The contact form uses the EmailJS service to transmit submitted information to the email address used by the website operator.

Personal data submitted through the form is used for the purpose of responding to your inquiry and communicating with you about the requested services.

7. Recipients and Service Providers

Personal data may be made available to service providers where this is necessary for operating the website, processing contact requests, providing technical services or complying with legal obligations.

Such providers may process personal data only to the extent necessary for the relevant service and subject to applicable data protection requirements.

The website currently uses EmailJS for the transmission of contact form submissions.

Other recipients may include competent public authorities, courts, professional advisers or other recipients where this is required or permitted by applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, to establish or maintain a contractual relationship, to comply with legal obligations, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

When personal data is no longer required, it will be deleted or otherwise securely disposed of, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

9. Direct Marketing

Where we process personal data for direct marketing purposes, we will comply with applicable data protection and electronic communications legislation.

You have the right to object at any time to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing purposes.

If you receive a marketing communication from us, you may unsubscribe or contact us using the details provided above.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for the operation, security and functionality of the website.

Where non-essential cookies or similar technologies require consent under applicable law, we will request the appropriate consent before using them.

You can also manage or delete cookies through the settings of your web browser.

11. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or unauthorised access.

Access to personal data is limited to persons and service providers who need it for legitimate business purposes or to fulfil legal obligations.
